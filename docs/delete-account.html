Delete your TaroLuna account

In the app

Open Profile, tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

Without the app

E-mail faceitall123qwe@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "TaroLuna account deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, saved readings and notes, push token and in-app reading balance, permanently. Records of purchases are kept by Google Play / App Store and RevenueCat as required by tax law.

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

Usuwanie konta (PL)

W aplikacji: Profil → Usuń konto. Bez aplikacji: napisz na faceitall123qwe@gmail.com z adresu konta. Usuwamy konto, rozkłady, notatki i saldo rozkładów w ciągu 30 dni. Subskrypcję anuluj osobno w Google Play / App Store.
