TaroLuna — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

TaroLuna is published by Tim Bazylewicz, Warsaw, Poland. Contact: faceitall123qwe@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: e-mail address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • App data: the readings you save (spread type, cards drawn, your notes), language, number of readings.
  • Purchases: subscription status and reading packs. Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details.
  • Notifications: a push token, only if you turn on the daily card reminder.

We do not use advertising, ad identifiers or analytics trackers, and we do not sell data.

Why

To run your account, keep your reading history, unlock what you paid for and send the reminder you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), performance of the contract).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and subscription status.
  • Google / Apple — sign-in and payments, if you use them.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.

How long

Until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile and reading history straight away. Purchase records may be kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to processing. Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or see how to delete your account. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO).

Children

TaroLuna is not intended for children under 16.
